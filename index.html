Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

RubySoftGames ("we", "us", or "our") developed Lunelle's Puzzle Adventure as a paid mobile game. This Privacy Policy explains how the game handles user information.

1. Information We Collect

Lunelle's Puzzle Adventure does not collect, transmit, or store personal information from users.

The game does not require users to:

  • Create or sign in to an account.
  • Provide their name or email address.
  • Provide a phone number or physical address.
  • Provide location information.
  • Provide payment information directly to the game.

The game is designed to be playable offline and does not operate its own online account system, cloud-save service, or user database.

2. How We Use Information

Because Lunelle's Puzzle Adventure does not collect or transmit personal or sensitive user information, RubySoftGames does not use such information for advertising, analytics, profiling, marketing, or other purposes.

3. Information Sharing

RubySoftGames does not sell, rent, or share users' personal or sensitive information with third parties because the game does not collect such information.

4. Third-Party Services

Lunelle's Puzzle Adventure does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or user-tracking services that collect personal or sensitive user information.

The game is distributed through Google Play. Google Play may independently process information in accordance with Google's own privacy policies and terms. Such processing is separate from the game's own data practices.

5. Data Security

Since the game does not collect or transmit personal or sensitive user information to RubySoftGames, there is no user personal data maintained by us through the game that requires storage or transmission security measures.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

RubySoftGames does not retain personal or sensitive user information collected through Lunelle's Puzzle Adventure because the game does not collect such information.

The game does not create user accounts or maintain personal data associated with user accounts. Therefore, there is no account deletion process required for the game.

7. Children's Privacy

Lunelle's Puzzle Adventure does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if the game's data practices or applicable requirements change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
